# Project Code of Conduct

This document outlines the standards and expectations for participation within the **imemix.github.io** community. We value diversity, respect, and collaboration, and we aim to foster an inclusive environment for everyone.

## Our Pledge

We commit to creating a welcoming atmosphere where all contributors can work and communicate respectfully. Harassment, discrimination, and abusive behaviors will not be tolerated.

## Guidelines

Positive contributions include:

* Being respectful and courteous in all communications
* Listening to and considering different viewpoints
* Providing constructive feedback and being open to receiving it
* Acknowledging mistakes and learning from them
* Helping others and contributing to the overall success of the project

Behaviors that are not acceptable:

* Insults, personal attacks, or demeaning comments
* Harassment, intimidation, or offensive language
* Discriminatory remarks based on race, gender, sexuality, religion,
  nationality, disability, or any other personal characteristic
* Sharing private or sensitive information without consent
* Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting and Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers directly via the repository's issue tracker or by email. Reports will be handled confidentially and reviewed promptly.

Consequences for violations may include:

1. **Warning**  A private message explaining the issue and asking for improvement.
2. **Temporary Restrictions**  Limited access to project resources or platforms for a period of time.
3. **Removal**  Permanent exclusion from the project and its communication channels.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related spaces, including issue tracker discussions, pull requests, mailing lists, forums, social media, and in-person events where participants represent the project.

## Acknowledgments

This policy is based on community guidelines developed by open-source projects and adapted to fit the needs of our repository.
